Engineered for Uncompromising Privacy

Every layer designed to prevent interception, tracking, and surveillance.

🆔

Pure ID-Based Access

No phone numbers. No emails. Accounts are identified strictly by randomly generated USR-XXXX-XXXX IDs. Zero contacts-upload metadata.

🔐

Client-Side E2EE

Direct Elliptic Curve Diffie-Hellman key exchange (Curve25519) combined with AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption. Messages exist as ciphertext only on the server.

📵

FLAG_SECURE Protection

Native Android window protection actively blocks screenshots, screen recorders, and obscures conversation views in the OS recent applications switcher.

⏳

7-Day Free Trial

Every newly generated account receives an automatic 7-day unrestricted trial calculated securely by the backend clock, tamper-proof against device adjustments.

🔑

Single-Use Recovery Key

Account credentials can be safely restored using a cryptographically generated high-entropy Recovery Code, securely salted and hashed on the server.

🛡️

Owner-Managed Licensing

Zero credit-card gateways or app store tracking. Premium access is provisioned via cryptographically signed license keys issued directly by the platform owner.
